# NOTES — personal website

## Design decisions (Round 0, approved plan, Oct 29, 2026)
- **Audience:** recruiters and hiring managers in banking and corporate finance; professors and mentors.
- **Feel:** professional, clean, trustworthy; navy, white and a touch of gray (Joselyn's choice).
- **The idea:** my career shown as a forecast. Past steps are solid "actuals"; graduation and the FP&A role are a dashed "forecast". Experience is laid out as ledger line items, like a budget sheet.
- **The one live thing:** the Actuals and forecast chart on Home. On desktop, hover or tab to a point to see its detail; the line draws once on load (off under reduced motion). On phones it is a vertical list with details always shown.
- **Palette:** ground #FFFFFF, ink #0F2341, accent #1F4E8C, surface #F2F4F7, muted #5B6573, rule #D5DAE1.
- **Type:** Newsreader (headings), Public Sans (body, tabular numbers). Sizes 14 / 17 / 22 / 34 / 54 px.
- **Mode:** light. **Shapes:** buttons and panels square; chart points round.
- **Honesty and privacy:** no invented numbers or dates; the Baystate merger detail is left out (may be confidential); no phone, address, student ID, GPA or transcript.
- **Placeholders:** every one starts with `[PLACEHOLDER]` and is shown in gray italics.
